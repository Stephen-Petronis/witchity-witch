# Witchity Witch Witch Which Way Do I Go

Starring the Witchy Witch of the Witch. A mobile-first Witch School game for the Monster Pizza Arcade.

**Play:** https://stephen-petronis.github.io/witchity-witch/

## The school day (one term)
1. **Monster Zapping** – trace the letter on each monster to zap it (30 seconds, how many can you get).
2. **Potions** – solve the colored math recipe to mix the right drops. Wrong mix = chicken.
3. **Broom Flying** – rail-shooter through the Dark Forest. Drag to steer, hold ZAP (bottom-left) to shoot.
4. **Mushroom Picking** – find the matching mushroom, as many as you can in 30 seconds.
5. **Fixing the Spellbook** – put torn pages back together to learn power-up spells.

A report card comes after every term. After 12 terms, Graduation Day tells you what kind of witch you are.

Each game has its own level: ★ = down a level, ★★ = same level, ★★★ = up a level. Progress saves in the browser.

## Tuning
Everything lives in `index.html`. Look for these blocks near the top of each section:
`CONFIG` (monster battle), `POTION`, `FLIGHT`, `MUSH`, `SPELLBOOK`, `SCHOOL`.
